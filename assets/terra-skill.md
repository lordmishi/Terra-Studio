# Terra Time Capsules: house rules

English prehistory documentary channel. Owner: Sigit (chat with him in Indonesian or English, follow his lead). He edits in Premiere Pro himself. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

## Audience
- 88% male. Viewers 45+ give 71% of watch time. US gives 67% of revenue.
- Write for an older, curious viewer: clear, confident, no slang, no hype words.

## Topic rules
1. Lead with a **giant prehistoric mammal predator**. This is what wins (Megistotherium "Miocene Butcher", "The Giant Baboon That Hunted Humans", Bear Dog, Andrewsarchus: 45k–96k views, CTR 5–7%).
2. Give it an angle: **a threat to humans or our ancestors**, or a **size / record claim**. Use the threat angle only when the evidence supports it, and say in the script when the evidence is debated.
3. Non-predator topics (plants, invertebrates, "evolution of X") have stayed under 500 views. Avoid them unless Sigit asks.
4. **Never repeat an animal already on the channel or already in the calendar.** Check the list below and Terra Studio's calendar first. Two ideas with the same kind of animal and the same story (for example two "hyena ate early humans" ideas) also count as a repeat.
5. Score each idea 0–5: mammal, predator, threat to humans, size claim, never made. Suggest only 4 and 5.

## Title rules
- Under 60 characters. Name the animal, or name what it was ("The Giant Baboon…").
- One hook: threat ("That Hunted Humans"), record ("Largest… Ever"), or a number ("3000 Pound").
- Clickable but honest. No "SHOCKING", no ALL CAPS words, no question-only titles.
- Give 5 options, best first.

## Thumbnail rules
- One animal, big, facing the viewer or attacking. A human or human ancestor for scale when the angle is a threat.
- 2–4 words of text, large, high contrast. Text must not repeat the title.
- Dark, natural palette. No red arrows, no circles, no fake "shocked face".
- Make 3 versions for YouTube Studio's own "Test & Compare". Sigit uploads and starts the test himself.

## Script rules
- **Length: 3,000–5,000 words.** Default 4,000. Sigit's rule: 4,000 words is about 15 minutes at most. Do not argue with this.
- **5 acts.** Each act ends with an open loop that the next act pays off.
  1. Cold open: a scene of danger in the first 15 seconds. No "welcome back", no "in this video", no subscribe request.
  2. Who it was: size, time, place, with numbers in digits.
  3. How it hunted or fought.
  4. The human angle: our ancestors and this animal, honest about the evidence.
  5. Why it vanished, and what it tells us. One short subscribe line at the very end only.
- Something to hold on to at least every 80 words: a number in digits, a size comparison, a moment of danger, or a question.
- Sentences average under 22 words, none over 40.
- Never invent numbers or quotes. When a number is uncertain, give the range and say so.

## Before upload (checklist)
- Facts: every number and claim in the script has a source; debated ideas are called debated.
- Title under 60 characters and matches the thumbnail angle.
- Description: first 2 lines repeat the hook with the animal name; chapters with timestamps; sources listed.
- Tags: animal name, genus, common names, era, "prehistoric", "documentary".
- Files: thumbnail 1280×720, subtitles, end screen, 3 thumbnails ready for Test & Compare.

## After publishing
- Check CTR after 48 hours. Below 4%: make 3 new thumbnails and start Test & Compare in YouTube Studio.
- Never swap thumbnails or titles automatically.
